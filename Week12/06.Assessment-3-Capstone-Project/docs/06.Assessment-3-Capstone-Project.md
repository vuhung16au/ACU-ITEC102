## 2. Assessment Criteria & Marking Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Distinction Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Cleaning & Wrangling** | 25% | Systematic removal of whitespace, regex extraction of currency, robust mixed-date parsing, clean left joins without data loss. |
| **Exploratory Data Analysis** | 20% | Correct calculation of quartiles, IQR, detection and justification of outliers, meaningful multi-variable group summaries. |
| **Visual Communication** | 20% | Clear, readable multi-panel Matplotlib charts adhering to Object-Oriented principles, proper titles, units, legends, and styling. |
| **Privacy & De-Identification** | 15% | Robust cryptographic salting, complete suppression of PII, appropriate coarsening, and quantitative verification of $k$-anonymity. |
| **Fairness & Ethical Governance** | 20% | Calculation of acceptance rates, Disparate Impact Ratio, mitigation of adverse impact under the 80% rule, completed EIA. |
