# Why Visualisation Matters

This module explores why visual inspection is critical in data science, demonstrating how identical summary statistics can hide radically different patterns (Anscombe's Quartet), and introduces Matplotlib's Figure and Axes architecture.

---

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize the dangers of relying solely on summary statistics (mean, variance, correlation).
- Explore Anscombe's Quartet and understand the role of Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Understand Matplotlib's Object-Oriented design (`Figure` canvas vs `Axes` plot areas).
- Detect outliers, non-linear relationships, and clustering through visual plots.

---
